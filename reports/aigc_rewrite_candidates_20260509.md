>

修改稿：

This study uses RUL prediction inside a coupled scheduling-maintenance loop rather than treating it as an isolated prediction task. Equipment degradation sequences are first processed by a GRU model to obtain normalized RUL estimates, and the resulting health signal is then included in the state inputs of both the maintenance agent and the scheduling agent.

On the maintenance side, three decision paths are compared: flat DDQN, hierarchical DDQN, and POMCP. They correspond respectively to direct action-value learning, separated judgment of maintenance necessity and maintenance type, and online planning based on particle belief. On the scheduling side, THDQN and PPO are used for comparison. THDQN selects a scheduling objective before choosing a dispatching rule, whereas PPO directly learns a policy distribution over six dispatching rules.

In the simulation experiments, six scheduler-maintainer combinations are evaluated under the same setting with `seed=42` and `EVAL_EPISODES=1`. The comparison focuses on total cost, tardiness cost, maintenance cost, and the lower bound of equipment health. The results show that `THDQN + hier_ddqn` performs better in this paired setting. This indicates that, after RUL information is introduced into the decision state, system performance is more closely related to the structural matching between the maintenance policy and the scheduling policy than to any single algorithm alone.
